October – On Now

ATEC Meeting Place 2026

Dates: 26–28 October 2026  |  Where: Hobart, Tasmania

Australia's tourism industry gathers in Hobart for ATEC Meeting Place 2026, with more than 500 tourism leaders, international buyers and operators meeting over three days. If you are flying in for the conference, Velaré Chauffeurs provides Hobart airport transfers and event transport, so you can move between the airport, your hotel and the conference venues without relying on taxis.

Chauffeur tip: Book your Hobart airport pickup in advance — conference weeks fill the city's cars quickly.

Cirque du Soleil – KOOZA Sydney 2026

Dates: Now showing until 3 January 2027  |  Where: Under the Big Top, The Entertainment Quarter, Moore Park

KOOZA is Cirque du Soleil's return to classic circus — daring acrobatics, the Wheel of Death, high-wire acts and live music, performed by an international cast under the Big Top at Moore Park. The Sydney season runs right through the holiday period, making it a perfect family night out or end-of-year celebration.

Chauffeur tip: Moore Park gets congested on show nights. Your chauffeur drops you at the door and waits nearby for the trip home — no parking, no walking in the rain.

November – Racing, Comedy and Big Days Out

Melbourne Cup Day Events — The Big Dance at Royal Randwick

Date: Tuesday 3 November 2026  |  Where: Royal Randwick Racecourse

Sydney's home of Melbourne Cup Day is Royal Randwick, where The Big Dance — a $3 million race — headlines a full day of live racing, fashion, food and entertainment, with the Melbourne Cup itself shown live on the big screens at 3pm. It is the one day of the year the whole city dresses up, so arrive like you mean it.

Chauffeur tip: Cup Day taxis are scarce and rideshare prices surge. A pre-booked chauffeur with fixed pricing means your ride home is sorted before the first race jumps.

Sydney EV Show

Dates: 6–8 November 2026  |  Where: ICC Sydney, Darling Harbour

Three days of the latest electric vehicles, new model launches, charging and home energy solutions, and free test drives — all under one roof at ICC Sydney. Whether you are buying your first EV, planning a fleet, or just curious about where driving is heading, this is the show to see it, drive it and compare it in person.

Chauffeur tip: Darling Harbour parking fills fast on expo weekends. Get dropped at the ICC entrance and picked up when you are done.

WBBL Double-Header — Sixers v Thunder & Sixers' Home Matches

Date: Sunday 8 November 2026, from 1:40pm  |  Where: North Sydney Oval

The WBBL Sydney Smash headlines a North Sydney Oval double-header: the Sydney Sixers take on the Sydney Thunder at 1:40pm, followed by Perth Scorchers v Melbourne Stars at 5:10pm — two matches, one ticket, one great afternoon. The Sixers are back at North Sydney Oval through November, facing the Renegades on 15 November, the Scorchers on 21 November and the Stars on 26 November, with the return derby against the Thunder at Drummoyne Oval on 28 November.

Chauffeur tip: Street parking around North Sydney Oval is limited on match days. A chauffeur is the easy way in and out for families and groups.

Just For Laughs Sydney

Dates: 9–22 November 2026  |  Where: Sydney Opera House, ICC Sydney and venues across the city

Australia's premier international comedy festival returns for its 14th Sydney edition. Two weeks of global comedy heavyweights, Australian favourites and new voices — including the All-Star Gala at the Sydney Opera House. With shows running late across multiple venues, it is the perfect excuse for a night in the city.

Chauffeur tip: Seeing two shows in one night? Your chauffeur can take you from dinner to the theatre and home again, on your schedule.

Royal Hobart Wine Show

Dates: 9–12 November 2026  |  Where: Hobart Showground, Tasmania

One of Australia's most respected wine competitions, the Royal Hobart Wine Show brings judges from across Australia and overseas to Hobart to find the country's best wines, with the trade tasting following on 13 November. Wine professionals and exhibitors travelling to Hobart can rely on Velaré for airport transfers and transport around the city throughout show week.

Chauffeur tip: If you are attending tastings or the Fine Wine & Dine Dinner on 4 December, let someone else do the driving.

F3 Polo Sydney

Date: Saturday 14 November 2026, 12:00pm–7:00pm  |  Where: Mission Fields, Centennial Park

Polo, reimagined. F3 Polo brings a fast, 3-a-side format to a compact grass arena at Centennial Park, blended with DJs, premium food and drink, VIP marquees and one of the most fashion-forward crowds in Sydney sport. It is Sydney's only premier polo event — and very much a dress-to-impress afternoon.

Chauffeur tip: There is no dedicated event parking and Centennial Park gets busy. Arrive by chauffeur and start the day relaxed.
